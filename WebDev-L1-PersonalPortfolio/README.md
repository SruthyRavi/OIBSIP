A responsive personal portfolio website built using HTML5 and CSS3 to showcase my profile, professional experience, technical skills, and projects.

## About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate with an interest in software development and quality assurance. Currently working as a Software Engineer Intern in a Quality Analyst role.

## Sections

- Home – Introduction and profile
- About Me – Educational background and interests
- Experience – Professional internship experience
- Skills – Technical skills and tools
- Projects – Featured projects
- Contact – Contact information and social links
